Our Story

Cloud Café is a neighborhood coffee shop that serves freshly prepared coffee, tea, and light snacks.

We provide a comfortable place for customers to work, study, or meet with friends — whether you prefer our cozy indoor seating or our relaxed outdoor area.

We also offer free Wi-Fi so you can stay connected while you enjoy your favorite drink. Come as you are, stay as long as you like.
